## Tips
- Generate 2 takes of each line and keep the more natural one. One line costs roughly 30–120 credits.
- If a tag is overacted, delete it and regenerate. The plain text alone sounds calm and natural.
- If the S07 → S08 handoff sounds disconnected, generate both as one line: paste S07 and S08 together, separated by `[pause]`.
- If the made-for-kids rule turns comments off, use `[happy] Exactly! Subscribe for tomorrow's "why" question!` for S10b instead.

## Syncing to the video in CapCut
1. Put the video shots S01–S10 on the main track in order.
2. Drop each mp3 under its shot, starting about 0.3 s into the shot.
3. Trim each shot to the length of its line plus about 1 s of breathing room. S03–S05 can run 1–2 s longer as visual moments.
4. For S10, put Blu's line first and Sunny's CTA right after it on the same shot.
